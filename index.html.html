Our mission

ARISE Treatment Center is a residential program for adult men. We help men who are medically stable gain the knowledge and strength to recover, with clinical care and a 12-Step foundation working together.

Our mission is to empower individuals and families to overcome behavioral health challenges and achieve lasting recovery through compassionate, evidence-based, and person-centered care.

We aim to be a leader in behavioral healthcare in Central Indiana, recognized for our clinical excellence, innovative practices, and unwavering commitment to the communities we serve.

How we can help

Structured care, every step of the climb

Holistic, person-centered treatment

Non-judgmental, trauma-informed care for men facing substance use, who may also carry medical or mental health concerns, built around each man's history and goals.

Structured living. Clinical care.

A CARF-accredited, ASAM Level 3.5 residential program for men who are medically stable. 28 to 30 days of clinician-led group and individual therapy with a 12-Step foundation. Acute detox is referred out.

Community connections

Clinical group sessions five days a week, daily recovery meetings, peer support, and case management. Recovery is stronger in community.

Aftercare, the climb continues

A step down to a lower level of care such as IOP or ASAM 3.1, a discharge and transition plan, and APEX follow-up to 180 days.
